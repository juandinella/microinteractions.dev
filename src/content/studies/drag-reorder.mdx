## Lift the row, nudge the rest

Press a row and move it: it leaves the list and follows the pointer, sideways too, with a larger shadow. The rows after its old slot move up and close the hole, so the list is one row shorter while you drag.

The gap at the pointer is only a nudge. The two rows beside it move 8px away from it, the next pair 5.6px, then 3.9px: each step is `0.7` times the last, or `8px × 0.7ⁿ` with n counting the steps from the gap. The delay is a stagger of `24ms × n`, so the two rows beside the gap start together and the rest follow. A 2px line in the gap shows where the row will land. The gap spreads outward from the pointer instead of the list swapping in blocks. The rule comes from Kubai's post, which also uses it on a dock of icons, with 10px.

The nudge and the slot translation share one `transform`, so a single transition carries both. A row that is still moving when the gap jumps retargets from where it is, so a fast drag doesn't queue up.

## Ease-out, no rebound

Every row moves for 250ms on `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, an ease-out: it starts at speed, so the rows beside the gap answer the pointer at once, then slows into place. There is no spring, because a list you reorder often shouldn't wobble. On release the lifted row takes the same curve and the nudge is removed, so every row lands together.

## Keep the DOM order until the drop

While you drag, the DOM order doesn't change. The lifted row follows the pointer with `translate` and the others move with transforms. After the drop settles, the script reorders the real nodes and clears every transform in the same frame, with transitions off. Nothing moves on screen, because each row is already where its new position puts it. Reordering on every slot change would swap the box under the pointer mid-gesture.

## Keyboard, touch, and reduced motion

Each row is a button. Space or Enter picks it up, the arrow keys, Home, and End move it, Space or Enter drops it, and Escape puts it back, also during a pointer drag. A polite live region announces the pickup, each move, and the drop, with the position out of the total. Keyboard moves don't animate: a held key repeats too fast, and the announcement and the focus state already say where the row went. Moving focus away drops the row where it is.

On touch the whole row is the handle, so `touch-action: none` stops the page from scrolling under a finger on a row. In a long page, use a narrower grip as the handle, or start the drag after a long press.

Two limits. Rows must be the same height, and the layout is measured once when the drag starts. If the page or a scrolling container moves mid-drag, the gap stops matching what you see, and there is no autoscroll at the edges.

With reduced motion the rows jump to their slots without the nudge or the stagger, the line only fades, and the lifted row still follows your hand. In forced colors mode a lifted row gets a `Highlight` outline.
