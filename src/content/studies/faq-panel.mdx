## One panel for every answer

An accordion usually gives each answer its own background, so one collapses as the next expands. This FAQ has a single panel. Choose a question and it slides from the open one to yours, resizing to fit the new answer.

The answers still expand and collapse, on the panel's clock, so the questions between them move with it. While any answer is open, the card reserves the height it has with the longest one open, and a shorter answer leaves that space at the bottom. Nothing below the card shifts. It only grows when the first answer opens and shrinks when the last one closes. Giving every answer the longest one's height would also hold the card still, but the panel would stop fitting its answer.

## Measured before and after

The panel's position and height depend on the answer's text and the container's width, so the script measures them. It uses FLIP: it reads where everything is, swaps the answers, measures again, and animates from the first values to the last with `element.animate()`.

The first reading comes from the screen, partway through a move included. Choose another question before the panel lands and it turns from where it is, on an ease-out so it doesn't pause at the turn.

## Text that travels with the panel

Both answers move the way the panel does. The old one slides 6px ahead and fades out with a 3px blur. The new one starts 6px behind, before the old one is gone, and lands with the panel.

The easing comes from a frame-by-frame recording of the FAQ this study adapts. It accelerates over the first third of the move and decelerates over the rest, with no overshoot, in 300ms instead of the original's 430ms.

To recolor the CSS version, set `--fqp-fg` on the component or any ancestor. It colors the questions, the lead of each answer, and the focus ring.

## Keyboard, find in page, and reduced motion

Each question is a native button. Tab moves between them, and Enter or Space opens one with the same move as a click. The arrow keys move focus between questions, and Home and End jump to the ends. Closed answers stay in the DOM with `hidden="until-found"`, so find in page can match them; in browsers that support it, a match opens its answer at once.

With reduced motion, the panel and the answers jump to their new state. The open question also shows its answer and a turned chevron, so the state isn't color-only.
